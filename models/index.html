The companies behind the AI

What they're worth: the price investors paid in their latest funding round, or the market value if they're public. A valuation is a bet on the future, not money in the bank.

Good to know

  • "Codex Astra" is not a separate model: it is GPT-6 Astra running inside Codex, OpenAI's coding app.
  • Meta's newest model, Muse Spark, is closed. Its last open models were Llama 4 (2025).
  • Alibaba's Qwen3.8 Max is closed; the big Qwen3.8 2.4T is its open-weight sibling.
  • Scores marked ≈ are estimates by Artificial Analysis.

Which ones can a high schooler use?

  • Claude (Anthropic): 18+ only. You'll see it in club demos, on a teacher's account.
  • ChatGPT (OpenAI): 13+, and under 18 needs a parent's permission.
  • Gemini (Google): try your school Google account first; personal accounts are 13+.
  • Open-weight models (Kimi, DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama, Gemma...) can run on your own computer. Each company's own chat app has its own age rules.
